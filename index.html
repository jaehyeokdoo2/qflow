Q-Flow resolves the expressivity–stability dilemma of flow-based policies through intermediate value learning, achieving stable and efficient training without backpropagation through the ODE solver.

Challenge: Expressivity vs. Stability Dilemma

Flow-based policies can model complex, multimodal action distributions that Gaussian policies cannot. However, gradient backpropagation through numerical ODE solvers leads to severe instability. Prior methods are forced into an uncomfortable trade-off:

  • FBRAC — maintains full expressivity via backpropagation through time (BPTT), but suffers from instability when behavior cloning constraints weaken.
  • FQL — achieves stability through one-step distillation, but sacrifices the representational power of flow models.

Idea

Flow-Consistent Value

  • The flow is deterministic, and therefore, the intermediate latent noise $x_\tau$ always yields the same action $x_1$.
  • Exploiting this property, it becomes natural to attribute the terminal utility back along the resulted flow trajectory
  • This invariance lets Q-Flow assign meaningful value estimates to intermediate states without fully rolling out to the clean action $x_1$.

[Placeholder] Figure — Flow-Consistent Value (deterministic path, value invariance along trajectory)

Intermediate Value Gradient

  • Notice that gradient information at the intermediate level is essentially about selecting the intermediate noise $x_\tau$ with the highest eventual outcome — steering the flow toward better actions before it fully commits.

Experimental Results (OGBench)

Standard Setting

Q-Flow achieves an average improvement of +10.6 percentage points over FQL, with notable gains of +31% on antmaze-giant, +19% on puzzle-3x3, and +25% on humanoidmaze-medium. It consistently outperforms baselines across Gaussian, Diffusion, and Flow-based policy categories.

+ various Offline RL techniques

Under more challenging protocols with larger ensembles, pessimistic value backup, and action chunking, Q-Flow achieves a +2.7% average improvement over the strongest baseline (QAM-E).
